What is commercial LED lighting?

Commercial LED lighting is energy-efficient lighting designed for business and public buildings such as offices, warehouses, car parks, retail and strata common areas. Compared with older fluorescent, metal halide or halogen fittings, LEDs use less power, last longer and can be paired with sensors and smart controls.

How much energy can commercial LED lighting save?

Savings depend on your existing fittings, operating hours and whether sensors are added. enLighten's LED solutions regularly deliver energy savings of up to 93%, with the biggest savings in spaces that are lit around the clock, such as car parks and stairwells.

How much does it cost to upgrade commercial lighting?

Cost depends on the number and type of fittings, mounting heights, the controls you need and your site's access requirements. Your ROI proposal sets out recommended products and pricing for your site, alongside the expected savings.

What is the return on investment for LED lighting?

The return comes from lower energy use, reduced standby consumption with sensors, and fewer replacements and maintenance call-outs. Because lighting accounts for around 20% of a commercial building's energy use, these savings add up quickly.

How long does it take to recover the cost of an LED upgrade?

Our LED lighting solutions typically pay for themselves in 1 to 2 years. Sites with long operating hours usually see the fastest payback. Your ROI proposal estimates the payback period for your building.

What are the benefits of smart commercial lighting?

Smart lighting adds motion sensors, daylight detection and dimming controls to efficient LED fittings. Lights run only at the level each space needs, which reduces energy use and wear while keeping areas safe and well lit.

Can motion sensors reduce commercial lighting costs?

Yes. Motion sensors dim lights to a low standby level when an area is empty and bring them back to full brightness when someone arrives. For example, Vico runs at 20% standby brightness, and Chamaeleon has standby power as low as 3W.

How does daylight detection work?

A daylight sensor measures natural light. When there is enough daylight, it holds the fitting off or dimmed, and it switches the light back on as daylight fades. It is ideal for perimeter car parks, warehouses with skylights and outdoor areas.

What lighting is best for commercial offices?

Offices need even, glare-controlled light at the levels recommended for each task under AS/NZS 1680. Efficient LED fittings combined with occupancy and daylight controls give comfortable light while cutting energy use. We can recommend fittings for your floor plate.

What LED lighting is suitable for warehouses?

Warehouses usually need high bay fittings matched to the mounting height. Tauro Black delivers up to 165 lumens per watt, comes in 30W to 240W options and has optional motion and daylight sensors, with IP65 and IK10 ratings.

What lighting solutions are available for car parks?

Car parks need durable fittings that can run 24/7. Chamaeleon has integrated motion and daylight sensors with emergency and non-emergency options, and Vico offers 600mm, 1200mm and 1500mm battens with motion sensing. Both are IP65 and IK10 rated.

Are enLighten products suitable for industrial buildings?

Yes. Our range includes IP65 waterproof and IK10 impact-resistant fittings for demanding environments such as warehouses, tunnels, construction sites and industrial facilities.

Do commercial LED lights need regular maintenance?

LEDs need far fewer replacements than older lamps, which reduces maintenance costs. Fittings should still be kept clean, and emergency lighting must be tested regularly under AS/NZS 2293.

What Australian Standards apply to commercial lighting?

The AS/NZS 1680 series covers interior lighting levels, AS/NZS 1158 covers lighting for roads and outdoor public areas including some car parks, and AS/NZS 2293 covers emergency lighting. The National Construction Code (Section J) also sets energy efficiency requirements for artificial lighting. Our team can help you choose compliant fittings.

Are government rebates available for LED lighting upgrades?

It depends on your state and site. In NSW, the Energy Savings Scheme's commercial lighting method ended for implementations after 31 March 2026, although some smaller businesses may still be eligible through other methods. Victoria's Victorian Energy Upgrades program continues to support commercial lighting. See our rebates, grants and incentives guide or ask our team.

Can existing commercial lights be replaced with LEDs?

Yes. Most fluorescent, metal halide and halogen fittings can be replaced with LED equivalents. We recommend replacement fittings that suit your existing layout and mounting, and your electrician installs them.

How long do commercial LED lights last?

Quality LED fittings last many years longer than traditional lamps. Rated life varies by product, so check each datasheet. All enLighten products come with a minimum 5-year warranty.

Does enLighten provide technical support?

Yes. Our team provides ongoing support and guidance, and we offer free technical resources including spacing guides, pole mount guides and IES files.
