enLighten /commercial-lighting landing page - static design prototype (draft for approval)

Open index.html in a browser. It's responsive: resize the window or use device mode to see tablet and mobile.

- assets/  Logo and compressed images taken from the live page. Use the full-resolution originals from the Umbraco media library for the build.
- The form validates in the browser and shows a prototype thank-you message. In Umbraco, keep the existing Umbraco Form (field GUIDs, workflows) and apply this markup/CSS to it.
- The savings calculator is indicative arithmetic only.
- Gallery and before/after photos are from enLighten's own site (the case study and car park pages).
- The images are compressed for this prototype, so the hero background looks soft at full width.
